![Leluja Original, with ornaments from the stylistic sets](Leluja-Original/images/leluja-original-header.png)

This repository is home to the Leluja type project, developed by Andrea Kulpas and Omnibus-Type, a Brazil/Argentina partnership. Its forms and counterforms are inspired by *wycinanki*, the Polish art of paper cutting.

The project is ongoing. For now, it contains **Leluja Original**.

## Contents

### [Leluja Original](Leluja-Original/)

A typeface family in four styles (Regular, Italic, Bold and Bold Italic) for printed texts that also performs well in titles, with 44 wycinanki ornaments in stylistic sets.

- Fonts in `otf/`, `ttf/` and `woff2/`
- [Specimen (PDF)](Leluja-Original/Leluja-Original_Specimen.pdf)
- [Full description, ornaments and OpenType features](Leluja-Original/README.md)
